# 中式英语病症清单（汉译英专用）

> 据 Joan Pinkham《中式英语之鉴》(The Translator's Guide to Chinglish) 提炼。例句均取自原书，A = 中式英语初稿，B = 改稿。

**何时读这份文件**
1. 做**汉译英**、产出英文初稿之前：先扫一遍"速查表"和各节"识别特征"，写的时候就避开。
2. **审校/润色**汉译英译文时：按文末"审校检查清单"逐项过，命中的条目回到对应小节看改法与边界。
3. 遇到政府文件、讲话、新闻稿、宣传材料等"公文腔"原文时必读，这类文本中式英语密度最高。

---

## 0. 总原则

- **三问**（全书收尾时的检验标准）：译文是否**清楚**（代词有明确先行词、无错位/垂悬修饰语、无过度压缩的表达）？是否**合乎逻辑**（重心正确、该平行处平行、该有连接词处有连接词）？是否**紧凑、轻快**（无多余或重复的词，抽象名词降到最少）？
- **黄金法则**（Gowers）："挑出那些能把作者意思传达给读者的词，只用这些词。"译者不是拿英文词去兑换汉字，而是"等值"而非"等量"地交换。
- **汉语里有两个词，从来不是英文里也要用两个词的充分理由。** 汉语可容忍的重复、修饰、范畴词，在英文里多半是累赘。
- **判断力优先于规则**：书中每一类"多余"都有例外。删之前要问：在这个语境里它是否承载了意思？是否只是因为原文有才被译进来？拿不准时**回查原文**——很多冗余其实是误读原文造成的，读准了冗余自然消失。
- **文本类型决定删改力度**：泛读的杂志/新闻稿可以大胆删；法律文件、领导人正式讲话、党代会报告、长期固定的政策提法要保守，宁可留一点冗余也不要丢失或改变意思（"meaning must have priority over elegance"）。
- **修改分步进行**：先处理最容易的（同义反复、多余修饰语），再处理名词化、句子结构、逻辑关系；改完再读，往往还能再改。

# 第一部分：多余的词（目标：简洁）

## 1. 多余的名词

### 1.1 冗余名词（意思已包含在别的成分里）
**识别**：名词的意思已被动词、时态、复数、其他名词蕴含。典型：the pace of（与 accelerate 重复）、in agriculture（与 harvests 重复）、in nature / in character / in color / in number（形容词本身就描述性质）、in the future / in the past / at present（时态已表达）、a series of / various kinds of / all sorts of（复数已表达）。
**改法**：直接删，连带删掉随之而来的冠词、介词。
- to accelerate the pace of economic reform → to accelerate economic reform
- these hardships are temporary in nature → these hardships are temporary
- we should adopt a series of measures to ensure... → we should adopt measures to ensure...

### 1.2 空洞名词
**识别**：删掉后意思不减反清。高危词：**situation**（常拖出 one where / in which）、**key**（常拖出 lies in）、realization of、building of、a subject that、the fact that（Strunk："应从每个出现它的句子中删去"）。
**改法**：删除并改写为直接陈述。
- at that time the situation in northeast China was still one where the enemy was stronger... → at that time the enemy was still stronger than the people's forces in northeast China
- the key to the solution lies in the curtailment of expenditure → the solution is to cut back on expenditure
- it is also necessary to put an end to the situation in which the leading organizations accompany the guerrilla units → leading organizations should stop accompanying the guerrilla units

### 1.3 范畴名词（中式英语中最常见的多余词）
**识别**："泛称名词 + of + 具体名词/动名词"，前者只是告诉读者后者属于哪一类：the work of、the cause of、the sphere/field/area of、the practice of、the factor of、the phenomena of、the business of、the task of、the objective/goal of、a relationship of、all cases of。
**改法**：删掉范畴名词。
- a serious mistake in the work of planning → a serious mistake in planning
- we must oppose the practice of extravagance → we must oppose extravagance
- promoting the cause of peaceful reunification → promoting peaceful reunification

**边界**：时间短语不总是多余——at present it is necessary to... 可能暗示"只是暂时必要"；范畴名词偶尔有实义：in accordance with the principle of self-reliance 要保留。

## 2. 多余的动词

### 2.1 弱动词 + 名词（名词化动作）
**识别**：make / have / give / provide / carry out / conduct / engage in / achieve / accomplish / realize / bring about / place / exercise / register / implement 等泛义动词，真正的动作藏在后面的名词里。被动式同理：approval should be given to...、solutions can be found...。
**改法**：把名词还原成动词。
- make an investigation of → investigate；make a decision to → decide to；make efforts to → try to
- have an influence on → influence；have respect for → respect；have adequate knowledge of → know enough about
- give guidance to → guide；provide assistance to → assist；exercise control over → control；place stress on → stress；register an increase → increase
- conduct a careful examination of → carefully examine；launch an attack against → attack
- accomplish the transformation of → transform；realize industrial modernization → modernize its industry
- approval should be given to all these projects → all these projects should be approved
- grain rationing was implemented → grain was rationed

### 2.2 弱动词 + 空名词 + 第三个词（"清嗓子"结构）
**识别**：动词和第一个名词都空，真正意思落在第三个词（名词/动名词/形容词/动词）上：reach the goal of、perform the task of、carry out the task of、adopt/follow the policy of、use the method of、assume the attitude of、adopt the principle of、achieve the objective of、have a ... effect on、take measures to、take steps to、take care to。
**改法**：把第三个词提升为谓语动词，前两个删掉。
- our efforts to reach the goal of modernization → our efforts to modernize
- our troops used the method of slow advance → our troops advanced slowly
- this measure will have a restrictive effect on the activities of speculators → this measure will restrict the activities of speculators
- in order to carry out the task of fighting on exterior lines → to fight on exterior lines（附：不定式前的 in order 常可删）

### 2.3 过度使用的引导性动词短语（公文高发）
**识别**：三组套话只起"拖延正题"作用：
- **make great efforts to** 类：make every effort to、try our best to、do our utmost to、do everything possible to、strive to、endeavor to、work hard to
- **pay attention to** 类：pay heed to、lay stress on、attach importance to、stress、emphasize
- **do a good job in** 类：make a success of、achieve success in、do successful work in、be good at
**改法**：直接写要做的动作。
- it is especially necessary to make great efforts to assimilate... → it is especially necessary to assimilate...
- the principal task at present is to do a good job in disseminating and applying... → ...is to disseminate and apply...
- all enterprises must pay attention to promoting excellent workers → all enterprises must see to it that excellent workers are promoted

**边界（重要）**：这些短语"四次里有三次是棉花填充，第四次可能有实义"。
- we must make great efforts in education 有实义；we must make great efforts to overcome this difficulty 可能在强调任务艰巨——保留。
- policy of：the bourgeoisie followed a policy of vacillating 自相矛盾（动摇就是没有政策）→ the bourgeoisie vacillated；但 China has always followed a policy of peaceful coexistence 必须保留。拿不准时（follow the policy of putting quality first）可改成 make it our policy to put quality first 以凸显"政策"。
- 机器分不清有意义和无意义的同一短语，这正是译者的价值。

## 3. 多余的修饰语

### 3.1 冗余修饰语
**识别**：意思已含在被修饰词里。valuable treasure、economically developed countries、all possible eventualities、all-round encirclement、final completion、foreign imports、new innovations；时间副词 now / previously / later（与时态重复）；**various**（复数已表达）；further 与 continue to 并用。
**改法**：删。这一类不必犹豫。
- more and more of our various construction projects → more and more of our construction projects
- We should continue to take measures to further improve... → We should continue to improve...

### 3.2 自明修饰语
**识别**：信息可想当然：national economy（默认就是国家经济）、proper readjustment、appropriately raise、adequate continuity、China's leading industrial city of Shanghai、clearly set forth、suitable techniques、the departments concerned。
**改法**：删。若原文确有具体所指，就把它说具体（suitable → suited to local conditions；fully → in all its aspects）。

### 3.3 强化词
**识别**：
- 弱词 + 强化词：extremely important、strong aversion、definitely、firmly believe、surely tell you。→ 换一个本身就强的词：essential / vital / crucial；detest；I am certain；I am convinced；I can assure you。
- 强词 + 强化词（更糟，强化词反而削弱原词）：great historic、serious chaos、firmly ban、strongly demand、totally abolish、thoroughly eliminate、completely smash、tremendous ... unparalleled、the broad masses、a tiny handful。→ 删强化词。
- **最常见的冗余强化词**：active/actively（努力不可能是"消极"的）、effective/effectively（control 不有效就不叫 control）、actual/true/real（facts, situation, conditions 本身就是真实的）、successful/successfully（accomplish 就是成功完成）。
- 强化词叠用反而引起怀疑：has convincingly proved 不如 has proved；is truly rising... genuinely growing 不如直接 rising / growing。美国宪法第一修正案只写 Congress shall make no law，不写 absolutely。
**改法**：删；若原文强调确需保留，用别的手段补：we are determined to enforce...，或换更强的单词（dissatisfied → indignant / outraged）。原书也举过 vigorously promote，但本 skill 对"大力""积极"一类的处理以 SKILL.md 总立场第 5 条为准：把力度转进强动词（step up、press ahead），不加 -ly 副词。

### 3.4 弱化词（qualifiers）
**识别**：quite、rather、relatively、comparatively、fairly、somewhat、basically、reasonably、a little；没有比较标准时滥用，显得畏缩、"明褒实贬"。a reasonably substantial book giving a comparatively complete history 在英文读者看来是冷淡评价。
**改法**：删。perhaps / maybe / possibly 通常改用情态动词 may / might。
- it is quite important to create suitable conditions → it is important to...
- perhaps it will take a decade → it may take a decade
- will lead to a possible loosening of credit → may lead to a loosening of credit
**边界**：有意的谦辞要保留（邓小平谈大别山战役 accomplished its tasks fairly well 是刻意低调）；事实上确未全部完成时 basically 必要（the rectification of the economy has basically been completed）。

### 3.5 套话修饰语（clichés）
**识别**：总是和某些词绑在一起出现、已失去意义的修饰语：arduous、imperative、conscientiously、diligently、resolutely、energetically、vigorously、unswervingly、persistently、unremittingly、earnestly、sincerely、very、fully、further。"强调太多等于没有强调"。
**改法**：首选删除；删了会丢意思时，换个新鲜说法避免单调（arduous → demanding；vigorous → intensive；further → renewed；resolute → determined）。即便是有力的词，同一篇里出现太多也会失效（书中润色者规定 resolutely 每页不超过两次）。
**边界**：领导人正式表态中的强调（carry out unswervingly the Party's basic line）、已固定的政策提法（the complete prohibition and thorough destruction of nuclear weapons，改了可能被国外解读为政策变化）——保留。

## 4. 同义反复（Redundant Twins）

**识别**：两个意思几乎相同的词并列。英文里没有两个词完全同义，所以正确的问题不是"有没有区别"，而是"**在此语境中区别是否重要，第二个词是否传达了原文中第一个词没传达的东西**"。三类：
1. 意思几乎相同：affluence and prosperity、rough and bumpy、contacts and dealings、corrected or solved、errors and mistakes、steps and measures、discussions and deliberations、shortcomings and weaknesses、strong and solid、firm and staunch、major and important、earnestly and sincerely
2. 一个包含另一个：best and thorough efforts、evaluated and comprehended、consider and work out、interfere with and undermine、promulgated and implemented、necessary and imperative、unprecedented and unique
3. 一个太空泛，无法与另一个区分：grow and develop、strengthen and improve、enhance and improve、healthy and rational、healthy and sound、the situation and circumstances、advances and developments

**改法**（按顺序尝试）：
1. 删掉一个：geographical surveys and explorations → geographical surveys
2. 用一个更好的词替换两个：faraway, distant areas → remote；be alert and wake up → rouse themselves；comfortable and wealthy → affluent
3. 两个词确实都需要但选错了：加词区分——conditions and environment → working conditions and social environment；或换搭配——grasp and implement Deng's remarks → understand and apply（remark 不能 grasp 也不能 implement）。加词前要确认没有把原文没有的意思加进去。

**边界**：确有区别时保留：voice their opinions and demands、protect their legitimate rights and interests、stability and security、examined and adopted / discussed and approved（不能默认会议审议后必通过）。高层正式讲话里的 support and assistance（可能暗含"道义支持"与"实际援助"之分）宁可保留；同一组词出现在普通社论里则可删一个。**拿不准就两个都留**。不要见到并列就机械删一个。

## 5. 一意两说（Saying the Same Thing Twice）

**识别**三种形式（以"按时到站"为模板）：
1. **简单重述**：we must arrive on time **and be punctual**。如 we must practice economy and reduce unnecessary expenditures；变体是循环句——用 in order to / so as to 连接同义两半：we must further strengthen the building of national defense **in order to enhance our defense strength**；control environmental pollution so as to protect the environment。
2. **不言自明**：we must arrive on time **in order to catch the train**。如 to increase soil fertility, we should promote scientific application of fertilizer（施肥当然为了地力）。
3. **正反镜像**：we must arrive on time **and not be late**。如 maintain our vigilance and never be off guard；pay close attention to the formulation of annual plans and not neglect it。

成因：汉语常不标出两部分的逻辑关系，译者自行塞进 and / in order to，但连接词解决不了"说了两遍"这个根本问题。

**改法**（按优先级）：
1. **回查原文找出更多/不同的意思**（最佳）：all advanced means of communication and communications equipment → means of transportation and communication（"交通"被误读为"通讯"）；两句之间真实关系可能不是 and 而是 in order to。
2. **删掉重复部分**，或用一个强表达替换两者：the change is an essential, not an insignificant one → the change is absolutely fundamental。
3. **换措辞**让重复不那么扎眼（权威文本不敢删时）：stability and unity → stability, solidarity。
4. **保留**：作者有意的强调（It is a good thing, not a bad thing, that the world is diverse）、需尊重的个人文风（毛泽东 remember their friends and not forget them）。

**边界**：保留应是例外。半句话告诉读者已知内容，会让读者怀疑整篇文本的价值。

## 6. 重复指称（Repeated References）

**识别**：同一事物在一句或一小段内被完整重复：机构全称反复出现、同一短语原样再现、同一个词在一句里出现三四次（如一句里四个 reform，且最后一个突然变动词）。
**改法**：
- **无必要的直接删**：imported vehicles totalled 960,000 units, compared with four million vehicles → 960,000 vehicles were imported, while four million were produced domestically
- **动词性的**：用 do so / do this / do the same / do likewise：for urban reform to show results → for urban reform to do so
- **名词性的**，四种缩短法：
  1. 去掉修饰语：The National Conference on Iron and Steel Production... → The Conference...；government bonds → bonds；或用 this/that/these/such/its：25 auto-parts joint ventures → 25 such ventures
  2. 用概括名词：these matters、the two、these two tasks
  3. 用代词：these lines and policies → they；a good suggestion → a good one
  4. 用首字母缩写（机构或反复出现的长词组，首次出现时先给全称并括注）：NPC、CASS、SAR、SOEs、TVEs、SEZs
- 即便不省字，也应避免近距离重复同一个词/同一个音（correct... correct → correct... right；resolutely 与 resolution 并现也要避免）。

**边界**：连贯的论述离不开重复指称，问题在于每次都"全称展开"。代词有歧义时，**宁可重复名词也不要歧义**（见 §8）。

# 第二部分：句子结构（目标：清楚、合逻辑）

## 7. 名词瘟疫（The Noun Plague）

**识别**：地道英语以动词为基础，中式英语以抽象名词为基础。一句中多个 -tion / -ment / -ity / -ance 名词由介词串联，常配被动语态。
- The prolongation of the existence of this temple is due to the solidity of its construction. → This temple has endured because it was solidly built.
- 高危句型："X 名词 + is vital / is important / constitutes / is an important prerequisite for..."、"refusal to... is bound to lead to loss of face"、"lack of ... and lack of ..."、play a ... role in。

**改法**（依次尝试）：
1. **名词 → 动词**：Adherence to Marxism is vital, and so is adherence to socialism → It is vital for us to adhere to Marxism and socialism；otherwise there can be no achievements in our work → otherwise we can achieve nothing
2. **名词 → 动名词**（还能逼出"谁做了什么"）：economic revitalization will be an arduous task → revitalizing the economy...；began its issuance of 100 million B shares → began issuing...；through cooperation with it → by cooperating with it
3. **名词 → 形容词/副词**：rich in elasticity → highly elastic；prove the correctness of these policies → prove that these policies are correct；guard against blindness in action → guard against acting blindly
4. **补词说透**（结构过度压缩时）：it is important to give full scope to the role of experts → ...to take full advantage of the knowledge and abilities of experts；a PC era characterized by the dominance of national brands → a PC era in which national brands will dominate the market
- 改写常连带把条件/因果显化：auctioned off as a result of its loss-making operation → because it was losing money；To participate in social labor is an important prerequisite for the emancipation of women → If women are to be emancipated, it is essential for them to participate in social labor；these documents have played a positive role in promoting... → have helped promote...

### 7.1 名词作定语 / 名词串
**规则**：单个名词作定语没问题（army officer、income tax、application form）；两个名词组成的常见复合定语可以（water conservancy project）；**原则上不超过两个名词叠作定语**（大写的机构名称例外，如 a State Planning Commission document）。不熟悉的组合即使只有两个词也要小心（work evaluation and workpoints allotment）。
**识别**：State key protection relic sites、power supply business regions、China's first National Urban Construction Archive Sector Achievements Expo——汉字可无标记并置，英文照搬就难懂甚至不可解。
**改法**：
1. 把其中一个名词改成动词/动名词/形容词，其余名词自然成为宾语：the method of work evaluation → the method of evaluating work；soil erosion control → to control soil erosion；China's meteorological system modernization drive → China's drive to modernize its meteorological system；the State assets management institutions → the institutions responsible for managing State assets；technology transformation → technological transformation
2. 插入介词：research topic selection → the selection of research topics；an important personnel and materials transfer center → an important center for the transfer of personnel and materials；the China Foreign Experts Employment Contract Disputes Arbitration Commission → China's Commission for Arbitration of Contract Disputes with Foreign Experts
3. 加连字符（只适合清晰的短组合，注意位置）：two noun combinations → two-noun combinations；mobile phone users → mobile-phone users（或 users of mobile phones）；避免 3-year-old teacher（应为 teacher of 3-year-olds）这类笑话
4. 补词说透：put forward rationalization suggestions → suggest ways of rationalizing production；a local specialties company → a company for the export of local specialties

**边界**：补词即解释，务必确认对原文理解正确。

## 8. 代词与先行词

**规则**：每个代词（人称代词 it/they/their、关系代词 which/that/who、指示代词 this/these）的先行词必须：①**明确写出**；②**无歧义**；③**距离近**；④**人称、数、性一致**。
**病症与改法**：
1. **先行词缺失**（汉语常省略主语，译者补一个空泛的 it / this 了事）：
   - The second question concerns close planting. It should neither be spread out too thinly... → Crops should be...（补出具体名词）
   - ...and demanded the withdrawal of U.S. troops. This became a broad-based people's movement（this 粘到 withdrawal 上）→ All this helped form a broad-based people's movement
   - Minutes of an interim telephone report to Mao Zedong during his investigation（his 被读成毛泽东）→ ...during an investigation
   - 原则：补出原文中靠语境推断的主语是译者的职责，但不能用含糊的 it/this 推卸。
2. **先行词有歧义**：state companies... grain traders... their regulating role → the traders' regulating role（**宁重复，勿歧义**）；同一句两个 it 指不同东西 → 改写使两个 it 指同一物。
3. **先行词太远**：中间插入了另一个同数名词，代词会被"就近吸附"——advisory teams... On June 17 they signed the Agreement → the two governments signed；关系代词默认紧跟在其先行词后：Man to take care of cow that does not smoke → Man who does not smoke to take care of cow。
4. **用 which / this 指代整个前句**：极易吸附到最近的名词上——took decisive measures to shatter the Gang of Four, in which he... played an important role（变成他在四人帮中起重要作用）→ ...to break up the Gang of Four, an operation in which he...；launch an unremitting struggle against bureaucracy, which is of great importance → ..., a struggle which is of great importance。办法：用一个概括名词作同位语充当先行词，或拆句改写。
5. **数不一致**（中国译者最常见的一致错误）：If each enterprise goes it alone, they will never... their products → it... its products；The Carter administration committed itself... but they adopted → but it adopted；集体名词前后单复数要统一。

## 9. 短语与从句的位置

### 9.1 逻辑：修饰语放错位置
**识别**：作状语/定语的短语、从句修饰了错误的成分，因为英文默认它修饰最近的词或主句动词。
- In an article celebrating the 28th anniversary of the founding of the CPC in 1949, Mao said（变成党成立于 1949）→ In 1949, in an article celebrating..., Mao said
- After years of effort, Gao said that soil erosion has been brought under control（变成高努力多年才开口）→ Gao said that after years of effort, soil erosion...
- developing and spreading improved orange strains among fruit growers → developing improved orange strains and spreading them among fruit growers
**要点**：时间/条件状语放在 that 从句的哪一侧，决定它修饰哪个动词。

### 9.2 重心：句末是最强调位置
**规则**：英语句子的强调位置是**句首和句末，句末更强**。最重要的信息放句末，次要放句首，附带细节放中间。汉语从句之间常无主从标记、看似并列，译者须先判断哪个是主句，再安排语序。从属分句一般前置，主句殿后。
- they reached the Dabie Mountains in late August（日期成了虎头蛇尾的结尾）→ In late August, after more than twenty days of exhausting marches and heavy combat, they reached the Dabie Mountains
- ...openly capitulated to the Japanese in December 1938 → In December 1938, ...openly capitulated to the Japanese（不要"大狗短尾"）
- the government will be in much better financial shape next year, despite another enormous deficit → despite another enormous deficit, the government will be in much better shape
- 附带好处：句末的信息自然引出下一句：Although we didn't work directly for the warlords, we used to serve the old society. So we cannot say we were not influenced by it.

## 10. 垂悬修饰语（Dangling Modifiers）

**总规则**：句首的分词短语、动名词短语、不定式短语、作定语的介词短语、单个形容词，其**逻辑主语必须就是主句主语**；句末的分词短语同样会挂到主语上。
| 类型 | Chinglish | 改法 |
|---|---|---|
| 分词 | Using either military or peaceful means, most of the remaining enemy forces were put out of action. | Using..., **we** put most of the remaining enemy forces out of action. |
| 分词（句末） | China has introduced much technology..., playing an important role in... | ..., **and these imports have played** an important role... |
| 动名词 | After gaining some experience, these measures will gradually be introduced... | **After we have gained** some experience, these measures... |
| 动名词 | by cutting the number of employees, the annual debt of the firm has decreased | by cutting..., **the firm has reduced** its annual debt |
| 不定式 | To lead China into the 21st century, efforts must also be made to... | To lead China..., **we** must also promote... |
| 介词短语 | With a favorable environment and preferential policies, any investment is assured of healthy returns. | With its favorable environment..., **Jiangxi Province** ensures that... / **Because Jiangxi has**... |
| like / as | Just like the Crowne Plaza, all rooms feature... ; As leaders, it is necessary to... | Like the rooms in the Crowne Plaza, all those in the Holiday Inn... ; As leaders, **we** must... |
| 形容词 | Tall and shy, Diana's stylish gowns were auctioned off | 无关信息就删：Diana's stylish gowns were... |

**两种改法**：①把逻辑主语改成主句主语（常要把被动改主动，"efforts must be made" → "we must"）；②放弃该短语，改成从句或独立分句。
**例外**：已虚化为介词/连词的分词可"悬垂"：generally speaking、judging from、barring、owing to、according to、provided、considering（作介词时）、concerning、regarding、assuming、allowing for、granted that。非母语译者应**保守**，只承认这类常见固定用法，拿不准就当分词处理、绑定正确主语。

## 11. 平行结构

**规则**：意义平行的成分用相同语法形式（词对词、短语对短语、从句对从句，且同类型）。四种场合几乎必须平行：
1. **并列连词 and / or / but 连接的成分**
   - water supply, security, and how to move people and trucks → water supply, security, and efficient movement of people and trucks
   - Timber is needed for construction and to withstand floods → to provide material for construction and to help withstand floods
   - integrating..., seeking truth from facts, and the mass line → ..., and following the mass line
2. **关联连词** both...and / either...or / neither...nor / not only...but also / not...but：两边结构必须完全对应。not only for their own localities but also in relation to prices → not only in relation to their own local conditions but also in relation to prices
3. **列表、编号条目、小标题**：First, the Party must keep... / Second, the unity... to be enhanced... / Third, strengthen... → 统一为 First, it must maintain... Second, it must enhance... Third, it must strengthen...；报告小标题统一用动名词（Seizing... / Accelerating... / Creating...）。
4. **比较**：比较的两项必须逻辑同类（房间比房间，不能酒店比房间）；no sooner...than、rather than 两边同形（was transferred rather than promoted，不写 rather than being promoted）。

**进阶**：并列名词同为抽象或同为具体（imperialism, bandits, landlords → imperialists, bandits, landlords）；并列动词同语态；短语内部结构一致（allocation of land, rent reduction, wage increases → allocation of land, reduction of rents, increase in wages）；有意平行的两句可用相同句型与措辞（only by doing x could the people do y / only by doing x could China do y）。
**边界**：强行统一后别扭，说明它们逻辑上并不平行，应换结构；不要给不平行的内容套平行形式（wounded once in an ambush, once in the leg, and once in the Netherlands）。名词与动名词混用有时可接受，靠判断。

## 12. 逻辑连接词

**规则**：汉语常把句子"并排放"，关系靠读者体会；英语读者需要明示。缺连接词的英文是"一盘散沙"。中国译者的风险是**连接词太少**，而不是太多（但也不要滥用 however / moreover）。
**常用类型**：递进（and, moreover, in addition, what is more）、转折（but, however, instead, on the contrary）、因果（because, since, thus, as a result, accordingly, so）、相似（likewise, similarly）、目的（to this end）、时间（then, meanwhile）、顺序（first, next, finally）、举例（for instance）、让步（of course, granted, to be sure）、强化（indeed, in fact）、重述（that is, in other words）、总结（in short）。
**病症与改法**：
1. **缺递进**：两句互相补强 → 加 And / Moreover / What is more（句首用 And/But 完全合法）。
2. **缺转折**（比缺递进更严重，读者会走错方向）：...there was no progress in the negotiations. In March 1983 Mrs. Thatcher wrote... → In March 1983, however, Mrs. Thatcher wrote...；I am a layman in natural science. As my trip... → But as my trip...；让步关系用 Of course..., but...。however 宜放在句首附近、两逗号之间。
3. **缺因果**（最关键，缺了读者会漏掉核心意思）：三句并列 → ...because they can no longer rely on..., and because their farmland was distributed...；→ Accordingly / Thus / As a result / Consequently / For that reason / So。
4. **可疑连接词**：
   - **while**：只宜表"同时"；表"虽然/而"时若有歧义就改用 although / whereas；绝不能当作高雅版的 and。
   - **with**（"万能接头"）：With the rapid increase of automobile production, Chen was promoted → When production began to increase rapidly, Chen was promoted；With professionals familiar with... these offices → Staffed by professionals...；With wide streets and a small population, the city is filled with gas stations → 两者无逻辑关系，拆成两个独立陈述。
5. **用错连接词**：最常见是该用 but 时用了 and / also（the public sector is the major sector and there are also other sectors → ..., but there are also others）；反之把递进误作转折（However, in many localities it has not yet been collected → ..., and in many places it has still not been collected）；and / or 混用（build and upgrade the highways → build or upgrade highways；neutral and sympathetic and even cooperative → neutral, sympathetic, or even cooperative）。
**判断方法**：选连接词前先想清两个意思的真实关系；可以从英文逻辑推断，也可以回查原文——两种方法常殊途同归。
